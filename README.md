# That's what Xi Said: A RAG Chatbot of President Xi Jin Ping's (习近平) speeches
## 习近平重要讲话计算文本分析

![Picture of President Xi Jinping's new year's address](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/524e46b7-9786-4f4d-8145-6fb45727f1d2)

Based off of my work with the OECD, "That's what Xi Said" is an regularly updating Retrieval Augmented Generation
(RAG) chatbot connected to a vectorized database of President Xi Jinping's speeches from 2022-2026. 

This chatbot is intended for researchers, journalists, and chinawatchers to search, contrast, and
compare Xi's public statements through the past decade. 
This tool is not a replacement for proper and rigorous text analysis, but it is helpful for gaining insights and orienting researchers towards specific texts.
